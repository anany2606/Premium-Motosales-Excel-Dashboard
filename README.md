# Two-Wheeler Premium Brands — Sales Analysis (Excel)

An Excel data analysis project on premium two-wheeler brand sales — BMW Motorrad, Ducati, Harley-Davidson, Triumph, KTM, Kawasaki, Aprilia, MV Agusta, Indian Motorcycle, and Honda Premium — across 2022–2026 and 10 countries, in INR with metric units.

##  Contents

Two_Wheeler_Analysis_Project_INR.xlsx
├── Dashboard          # KPIs, charts, and summary tables
└── Two_Wheeler_Data   # 10,000-row raw dataset

##  Dataset

10,000 rows spanning 2022–2026 (quarterly), 47 models, and 10 countries. Columns include Brand, Model, Category, Year, Quarter, Country, Engine_CC, Price_INR, Units_Sold, Mileage_kmpl, Top_Speed_kmph, Customer_Rating, and Warranty_Years.

## Dashboard

Built entirely with Excel formulas (SUMIF, AVERAGEIF, SUMPRODUCT) and native charts — no add-ins required:

- **KPI cards**: Total Units Sold, Total Revenue, Average Price, Average Customer Rating
- **Brand-wise table**: Units Sold, Revenue, Avg Price, Avg Rating, Market Share % — with bar and pie charts
- **Year-wise trend table**: Units Sold and Revenue by year — with a line chart
- **Category-wise mix table**: Units Sold and Avg Rating by category — with a bar chart

##  Business Questions Answered

- Which brand leads in volume vs. revenue?
- How is the market trending year over year?
- Which two-wheeler category (Sports, Cruiser, Adventure, etc.) is most popular?
- How does customer satisfaction vary by brand and category?

## 📌 Note
All data is synthetically generated for portfolio/practice purposes and does not represent real sales figures. Brand and model names are real public product names, included for realism.
